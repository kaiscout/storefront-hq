## Channel rules

- [Upwork requires pre-contract communication to remain on-platform](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051749534-How-to-keep-your-contact-information-safe-on-Upwork). Do not include Gmail, Cal, a phone number, or an external payment link in these proposals.
- [Freelancer directs communication and payment through its messaging and milestone system](https://www.freelancer.com/support/General/communicating-or-paying-outside-freelancer-com). Do not move these buyers to Gmail, Cal, or Stripe.
- A new marketplace account may require identity or payment verification. Complete that verification truthfully; do not create burner profiles or claim experience you cannot substantiate.
