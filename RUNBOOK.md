# Seven-day revenue sprint

The target is one **£895** sale. That clears US$1,000 at ordinary recent exchange rates, but neither the exchange rate nor a sale is guaranteed. A realistic downside is £0.

## Offer

**48-Hour Shopify Peak-Season Storefront QA + 30-Day Regression Watch**

- Up to 250 public pages
- 12 selected, reproducible findings
- Exact route, mobile screenshot, timestamp, reproduction steps and severity
- Redirect CSV and copy-ready fixes for the priority issues
- 20-minute handoff through Cal where channel rules permit
- Daily observation of 10 agreed public routes for 30 days
- Fewer than eight actionable findings: full refund
- No backend access, retainer or implementation upsell

## Buyer filter

Only pursue a request when all of these are true:

1. The buyer explicitly asks for Shopify QA, broken-path, CRO, speed or peak-season help.
2. The post is under 72 hours old.
3. The live store appears to have 50–500 product/collection pages.
4. The likely budget is at least £750/US$1,000.
5. A public five-page preview produces at least one reproducible finding.
6. The reply stays in the request’s permitted channel. Marketplace requests stay on-platform, including scheduling and payment when required.

Skip pre-revenue dropshippers, regulated products, full rebuilds and anyone asking for guaranteed uplift.

## Daily plan

- **Day 0:** Complete the launch gate: verified operator identity, dedicated business inbox, Firecrawl, Cal, payment processor and deployed environment secrets.
- **Days 1–2:** Review 15–20 fresh intent candidates daily. Qualify only strong matches. Submit 8–10 evidence-led proposals daily in the allowed channel.
- **Days 3–4:** Continue new proposals. Answer interested replies promptly, run fit calls, and send the tracked payment link only after scope confirmation.
- **Day 5:** Close and collect one £895 payment. Start the crawl as soon as payment is confirmed.
- **Days 6–7:** Validate the automated evidence, select the top 12 findings, deliver the fix pack and run the handoff.

## Funnel math

Forty tailored proposals × 10% meaningful reply × 50% booked call × 50% close = one sale. This 2.5% proposal-to-sale assumption is aggressive for new accounts; treat it as a target, not a forecast.

## Proposal rule

Reference the buyer’s own request and exactly one verified issue. Never invent a revenue-loss number.

> You mentioned pre-peak QA. Your `/collections/...` page currently links to `/products/...`, which returns a 404 on mobile. I can deliver the complete evidence ledger and fix pack within 48 hours.

## Stop conditions

- Stop a channel immediately after any objection or opt-out.
- Pause the sprint if replies allege spam, the source did not actually invite proposals, or channel terms prohibit the contact.
- Never rotate accounts or increase volume to evade provider limits.
- Do not start work until payment clears or the marketplace escrow shows funded.
